## F1 "it can't see my actual health" -> FIXED
  mc hearts LyonWGF => 'health 20 (observed)' (previously always '(estimated)')
  Root cause: entity.metadata is a raw-value array; health sits at the
  registry metadataKeys slot for 'health' (=9 on 1.21.11), but the reader
  scanned the array for {key:'health'} objects — never matched. The value
  was on the wire the whole time (verified: player 20, hurt player 13.33,
  cow 10, damaged skeleton 9). Fix: resolve the slot through the registry
  (perceive.js healthFromMetadata); the mock now mirrors the wire (steve
  carries metadata[9], attack() moves the slot) so drift stays visible.

## F2 "wander gets stuck on the current level of y" -> FIXED
  Six-part root cause, all pinned by offline tests (mutation-checked):
  - _randomReachable anchored on stale home Y and proposed targets across
    cliffs ('goal is below, stepping down dy=-12 maxDrop=3' in the log);
    now anchors on the bot and only proposes walkable targets (dy -3..+2).
  - Recovery ladder had no descent step: it could only jump / climb UP /
    dig. New 'goal is below' phase walks off the ledge with a safe 3-block
    budget; an 'escape to open ground' phase handles 1x1 pillars and
    treetops (reproduced offline: climbOut held forward for 2.6s on a
    pillar, now escapes); a dig-straight-down last resort clears canopies.
  - The attempt counter reset on the recovery's own micro-movement, so the
    ladder oscillated attempt=1->2->1->2 forever (live log). It is no longer
    forgiven while a recovery is in flight, and progress now means beating the
    closest approach so far (oscillation cannot buy back the budget).
  - Wander re-rolls a fresh target when the ladder reaches its cap instead
    of stopping entirely; the dig phase now AWAITs the dig (fire-and-forget
    + 900ms sleep re-dug the same spruce_log forever — observed live).
  Live: bot descended y=70 -> 63 during wander; 'recovered by descending'
  and 'recovered by jumping' logged; treetop spawn eventually dug out.
